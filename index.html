About Us

University Church at Africa Nazarene University brings students, staff and the wider community together to worship, grow in Christ, and serve.

Mission

Faith in community

We exist to know God, love God, and serve God by making Christlike disciples who engage our campus and community with care.

Vision

Welcoming every person

To be a warm and active church where everyone finds belonging, encouragement, and spiritual growth in Jesus.

Beliefs

Grounded in holiness

We follow the Wesleyan-Holiness tradition, trusting Scripture, grace, and the transforming work of the Holy Spirit.

Modern church, timeless truth

Our community balances strong worship, compassionate outreach, and youth-friendly discipleship in a way that feels fresh and inviting.
