Our History

University Church of the Nazarene traces its roots to the founding of the Church of the Nazarene in 1908 in Pilot Point, Texas. From that beginning, our congregation has been shaped by a deep commitment to holiness, mission, and practical compassionate ministry.

As part of the Wesleyan-Holiness family, we value a faith that changes hearts and actions. Our story is one of faithful worship, disciplined discipleship, and service that reaches into the campus and wider community.

Our Beliefs

  • We believe salvation is offered through Jesus Christ, and Scripture is the authority for faith and practice.
  • We affirm holiness as a life shaped by God’s grace, with sanctification and holy living at the center of our discipleship.
  • We follow a Wesleyan-Holiness heritage that calls us to grow in Christ together, serving in community and supporting one another.
  • Our faith is expressed in mission and service, caring for neighbors with compassion while sharing the hope of the gospel.
